answer:
  - "The minimum payment is the smallest amount the card company accepts by the due date to keep your account in good standing."
  - "It's a required payment, not a plan to clear the balance. How it's worked out varies by card; often it's a small share of the balance plus that month's interest, so it falls as the balance falls. In the example below, paying only the minimum takes about 15 years and costs more in interest than was borrowed."

## Post 2: Calculate Net Worth

### Title

EN: How to Calculate Your Net Worth in 10 Minutes

### Description

EN: A step-by-step guide to listing everything you own and everything you owe, with a free multi-currency calculator for assets spread across countries.

### TL;DR

EN: List all your assets (bank balances, investments, property, vehicles) at current value. List all your liabilities (mortgage, loans, credit cards) at outstanding balance. Subtract liabilities from assets. That's your net worth. Update it monthly to see the trend.

Actualízalo una vez al mes para ver la tendencia.

### Article Body

EN:

You know that [net worth equals assets minus liabilities](/blog/what-is-net-worth/). But how do you actually sit down and figure out your number?

Here's a method that takes about ten minutes. Grab a pen, open your banking apps, and let's go.

## Step 1: List your assets

Go through every account and asset you have. Use current balances, not what you originally paid.

**Cash and bank accounts:**
- Checking account balance
- Savings account balance
- Any cash on hand

**Investments:**
- Stock portfolio at current market value
- Mutual funds and ETFs
- Pension or retirement account balance
- Crypto at current value

**Property:**
- Real estate at current estimated market value, not purchase price
- Vehicle: check a used car site for your model and year

**Other:**
- Valuable items you could realistically sell

If you're unsure about a value, estimate conservatively. A number that's roughly right is infinitely better than no number at all.

## Step 2: List your liabilities

Now list everything you owe.

- Mortgage balance: the remaining principal, not the original loan
- Student loans
- Car loan
- Credit card balances
- Personal loans from friends or family
- Any other debt

Use the current outstanding balance, not the monthly payment. If your mortgage has €180,000 remaining, that's your liability, regardless of what you pay each month.

## Step 3: Do the math

Add up all assets. Add up all liabilities. Subtract.

That's your number.

It uses live mid-market rates and shows you how each currency contributes to the bottom line, so a strong dollar or weak rupiah does not quietly distort what you actually own.

## Common surprises

When people do this exercise for the first time, a few things tend to catch them off guard.

**"My car is worth less than I thought."** Cars depreciate fast. That €25,000 car you bought three years ago might be worth €14,000 now. Use current market value.

**"My net worth is negative."** If you have a large student loan or mortgage, this is quite common, especially in your twenties and thirties. A negative net worth isn't failure; it's a starting point.

**"I have more than I realized."** Small accounts add up. That old savings account with €800, the pension your employer contributes to. People often forget these.

## The key is consistency

The first calculation is the hardest. After that, it gets easier. Update your number once a month and watch the trend. That trend, whether growing, stable, or shrinking, tells you more than the absolute value ever could.

## Sorpresas comunes

Cuando la gente hace este ejercicio por primera vez, algunas cosas suelen sorprender.

**"Mi coche vale menos de lo que pensaba."** Los coches se deprecian rápido. Ese coche de 25.000 euros que compraste hace tres años puede valer 14.000 euros ahora. Usa valor de mercado actual.

**"Mi patrimonio neto es negativo."** Si tienes un préstamo estudiantil grande o una hipoteca, esto es bastante común, sobre todo en tus veinte y treinta años. Un patrimonio neto negativo no es un fracaso; es un punto de partida.

**"Tengo más de lo que creía."** Las cuentas pequeñas suman. Esa antigua cuenta de ahorro con 800 euros, la pensión a la que contribuye tu empleador. La gente suele olvidarlas.

## La clave es la constancia

El primer cálculo es el más difícil. Después se vuelve más fácil. Actualiza tu número una vez al mes y observa la tendencia. Esa tendencia, ya sea creciente, estable o descendente, dice mucho más que el valor absoluto.

EN: What should I include as assets when calculating net worth?

ES: ¿Qué debería incluir como activo al calcular el patrimonio neto?


EN: Include anything you own that has resale or cash value: checking and savings balances, cash on hand, stock and fund portfolios at current market value, pension and retirement account balances, crypto at current value, real estate at its current estimated market value, vehicles priced from a used-car site, and any valuables you could realistically sell. Use current values, not what you paid. Do not include your salary, your degree, or future earnings; those are not assets you own today. Small forgotten accounts, like an old savings account or an employer pension, often add up to more than people expect.

EN: Should I use the loan balance or the monthly payment for liabilities?

ES: Para los pasivos, ¿uso el saldo del préstamo o la cuota mensual?


EN: Use the current outstanding balance, not the monthly payment. Your liability is the total amount you still owe, regardless of how you repay it. If your mortgage has €180,000 of principal remaining, that €180,000 is the liability, even though you might only pay €900 a month. The monthly payment matters for budgeting and cash flow, but for net worth you need the full remaining balance of every debt: mortgage principal, student loans, car loan, credit card balances, and personal loans.

ES: Usa el saldo pendiente actual, no la cuota mensual. Tu pasivo es el importe total que todavía debes, independientemente de cómo lo devuelvas. Si a tu hipoteca le quedan 180.000 euros de principal, esos 180.000 euros son el pasivo, aunque pagues solo 900 euros al mes. La cuota mensual importa para el presupuesto y el flujo de caja, pero para el patrimonio neto necesitas el saldo restante completo de cada deuda.


EN: How do I calculate net worth with assets in different currencies?

ES: ¿Cómo calculo el patrimonio neto con activos en distintas divisas?


EN: Convert every asset and liability into one base currency before subtracting. Pick a functional currency (usually the one you live and spend in), then convert each foreign balance using a recent exchange rate. Add up all assets in that single currency, add up all liabilities, and subtract. The risk is that a strong or weak currency quietly distorts your total. A multi-currency net-worth calculator handles the conversions with live mid-market rates and shows how each currency contributes, so you see one clean total rather than a misleading mix.

ES: Convierte cada activo y pasivo a una sola divisa base antes de restar. Elige una divisa funcional, normalmente aquella en la que vives y gastas, y convierte cada saldo extranjero con un tipo de cambio reciente. Suma todos los activos en esa divisa, suma todos los pasivos y resta. El riesgo es que una divisa fuerte o débil distorsione el total sin que lo notes. Una calculadora de patrimonio neto multidivisa hace las conversiones con tipos medios de mercado en vivo y muestra cuánto aporta cada divisa.


EN: How often should I update my net worth calculation?

ES: ¿Con qué frecuencia debería actualizar mi patrimonio neto?


EN: Update it once a month. The first calculation is the hardest because you are gathering every balance and value for the first time; after that, refreshing the numbers takes only a few minutes. A monthly cadence is frequent enough to reveal the trend without reacting to short-term market noise. Take the snapshot on roughly the same date each month and record it somewhere you can compare over time. Whether the number is growing, stable, or shrinking tells you far more about your finances than any single reading ever could.

ES: Actualízalo una vez al mes. El primer cálculo es el más difícil porque estás reuniendo todos los saldos y valores por primera vez; después, refrescar los números toma solo unos minutos. Una cadencia mensual es suficiente para ver la tendencia sin reaccionar al ruido de mercado de corto plazo. Toma la foto en una fecha parecida cada mes y guárdala en algún lugar donde puedas comparar.

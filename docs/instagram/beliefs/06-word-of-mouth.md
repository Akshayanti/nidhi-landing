# Made in Prague, Powered by Word of Mouth

## Caption

nidhi is made in Prague and powered by word of mouth.

No VC growth machine. No billboards. No retargeting campaign chasing you around the internet.

If the blog helped, share it. If a tool was useful, tell someone. If the idea matters, repost it.

For an independent project, that is not a small thing. That is the engine.

# What Is Net Worth and Why Does It Matter?

## Caption

Most people measure their finances by their salary. But salary is income, not wealth. Your net worth is the number that actually tells you where you stand.

Swipe through to learn what net worth really means, why it matters more than your paycheck, and how to start thinking about it -- even if it's negative right now.

## Slide 1

What is net worth?

(And why it matters more than your salary)

---

## Slide 2

If someone asked how you're doing financially, what would you say?

Most people answer with their salary.

**"I make €3,500 a month."**

---

## Slide 3

But salary tells you what comes in.

**Not what stays.**

---

## Slide 4

**Net Worth =**
**Everything You Own**
**minus**
**Everything You Owe**

---

## Slide 5

**What you own (assets):**

- Cash & savings
- Investments
- Property
- Vehicles

---

## Slide 6

**What you owe (liabilities):**

- Mortgage
- Student loans
- Credit card balances
- Any other debt

---

## Slide 7

Someone earning €6,000/month who spends €5,800 is building less wealth than someone earning €3,000 who saves €500.

**Income is vanity. Net worth is reality.**

---

## Slide 8

**"But my net worth is negative..."**

If you have student loans or a mortgage, that's completely normal -- especially in your 20s and 30s.

**A negative net worth isn't failure. It's a starting point.**

---

## Slide 9

**The four states of net worth:**

1. Positive & growing -- building wealth
2. Positive & shrinking -- something's off
3. Negative & improving -- right track
4. Negative & worsening -- time to act

---

## Slide 10

Net worth isn't about being "rich."

It's about knowing where you stand so you can make better decisions.

**You can't improve what you don't measure.**

---

## Slide 11

**Start here:**

1. List what you own (current values)
2. List what you owe (outstanding balances)
3. Subtract
4. Repeat monthly

---

## Slide 12

Your salary tells you what comes in.

Your net worth tells you what stays.

**Track the one that actually matters.**

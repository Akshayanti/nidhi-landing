## Slide 2

Insurance transfers catastrophic risk from you to an insurer

You pay a small, predictable amount (the premium)

In return, they cover large, uncertain losses

Trade-off: certain cost now vs protection against devastating loss later

---

## Slide 3

>> **The five types that matter**

What you need depends on what your country's public systems already cover. The question is: where are the gaps?

1. Health: covering what public systems don't
2. Life: income for your dependents
3. Property: your home and belongings
4. Disability: replacing lost income if you can't work
5. Liability: covers damage you cause to others

---

## Slide 4

>> **The one most people skip: disability**

Your ability to earn is your most valuable asset

A 30-year-old earning €40,000/year will earn €1,000,000+ over 25 years

A long-term disability cuts off that entire stream

Premium Cost: typically €30-€80/month

---

## Slide 5

>> **What it costs**

The prices are typical, but they vary by country

- Health (supplemental): €50-€300/month
- Life (term, €200k): €15-€40/month
- Renter's / contents: €10-€25/month
- Disability: €30-€80/month
- Personal liability: €5-€15/month

Total: €110-€460/month depending on your needs

---

## Slide 6

>> **Insurance + emergency fund = a team**

- More coverage = smaller emergency fund needed
- Less coverage = bigger emergency fund needed

---

## Slide 7

>> **Combining insurance with emergency fund**

If you have

- Comprehensive insurance: 3 months of emergency fund may be enough
- Moderate coverage: 4-6 months is a common guideline
- Minimal coverage: 6+ months, and it still may not last

---

## Slide 8

>> **When insurance makes sense**

- The loss would be large enough to disrupt your finances
- You couldn't cover it from savings without derailing your goals
- The probability is low but the impact is high

Rule of thumb: insure what you can't afford to lose

---

## Slide 9

>> **When it doesn't**

- The loss is small enough to cover from savings
- The premium is high relative to the payout: extended warranties on electronics, gadget insurance, travel insurance for a €200 trip

Self-insure the small stuff

---

## Slide 10

>> **How insurance protects your net worth**

- Property insurance protects your assets
- Liability insurance prevents new liabilities
- Disability insurance protects your income stream
- Life insurance protects your dependents

It doesn't grow your wealth
It prevents one event from destroying it

---

## Slide 11

>> **What you can do**

1. List every policy you have: what it covers, what it costs
2. Identify gaps (disability? renter's? liability?)
3. Size your emergency fund to your coverage level
4. Don't insure small losses: save insurance for the big ones
5. Review annually as your life changes

## Slide 2

>> **What is a credit score?**

A credit score is a number that summarizes how reliably you've handled borrowed money

Higher score = lower perceived risk = better loan terms

It's based on your past behavior, not your income

---

## Slide 3

>> **What goes into your score**

Every country's system is different, but these factors matter almost everywhere:

1. Payment history (universally most important)
2. Outstanding debt and credit utilization
3. Length and stability of credit history
4. Types of credit (mix of cards, loans, etc.)
5. Recent credit activity

---

## Slide 4

>> **The interest rate gap**

€200,000 mortgage over 25 years:

- Excellent credit: 3.2% = €90,800 total interest
- Good credit: 3.8% = €110,100
- Average credit: 4.5% = €133,500
- Poor credit: 5.5% = €168,500

Same house. Up to €77,000 difference

---

## Slide 5

>> **Why it matters without debt**

- Landlords check credit for rental applications
- Some insurers use credit in pricing
- Phone and utility contracts may require credit checks
- If you ever need to borrow, good credit means fair terms

A good score keeps options open

---

## Slide 6

>> **Credit utilization**

Credit card limit: €5,000
Using €4,500? That's 90% utilization - a red flag

A common guideline: stay below 30%

High utilization signals financial stress to lenders

---

## Slide 7

>> **How to build good credit**

1. Get a credit card, use it for small purchases
2. Pay the full balance every month (not the minimum)
3. Keep utilization below 30%
4. Don't close your oldest account
5. Avoid applying for multiple products at once

---

## Slide 8

>> **Common myths**

- "Checking my own score will lower it"
No. Only new credit applications affect it

- "I need to carry a balance to build credit"
No. Paying in full every month works just as well

- "No history means a good score"
No. No history can be as problematic as bad history

---

## Slide 9

>> **The hidden cost**

Your credit score doesn't appear on your balance sheet

But it determines the interest rate on every loan you take

A better score means less money going to the bank and more staying with you

---

## Slide 10

>> **What you can do**

1. Check your credit report (most countries offer free annual access)
2. Start building now if you have no history
3. Pay on time, every time
4. Think long-term: credit scores reward consistency

---

## Slide 11

>> **Bottom Line**

Your credit score is one piece of your financial defense

The other is protecting what you've built from sudden, unexpected loss

>> **Self-knowledge check**

Be honest and try to answer these questions. Revise if unsure:
- What is net worth and how do you calculate it?
- What counts as an asset? What counts as a liability?
- How do you get out of debt: snowball or avalanche?
- Why do some assets grow in value and others shrink?

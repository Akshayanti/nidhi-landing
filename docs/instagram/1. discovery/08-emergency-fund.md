# The Emergency Fund: Your First Financial Safety Net

## Caption

Before you invest. Before you pay extra on debt. Before anything else -- build a buffer.

An emergency fund is the foundation that keeps the rest of your financial plan from collapsing. Here's how to build one, how much you need, and where to keep it.

## Slide 2

>> **What is an emergency fund?**

An emergency fund is cash set aside for unexpected, necessary expenses

Not a holiday, Not a sale

A genuine emergency you can't predict and can't ignore

---

## Slide 3

>> **Why build it?**

Without an emergency fund, every surprise forces a bad choice:

- Credit card at 20%+ interest
- Skip rent or mortgage
- Sell investments at a loss
- Borrow from family

---

## Slide 4

With an emergency fund, the same expense is just an inconvenience

Pay for it. Replenish the fund. Move on

No debt. No panic. No long-term damage

---

## Slide 5

Think of it as your financial shock absorber

It doesn't prevent the bump. 

It stops the bump from breaking the suspension

---

## Slide 6

>> **Target #1: Mini emergency fund**

One month of salary

Covers small surprises: car repairs, medical copays, home fixes

Build this first, before anything else

---

## Slide 7

>> **Target #2: Full emergency fund**

Three to six months of essential expenses

Rent, food, insurance, minimum debt payments, utilities
Not dining out, not entertainment, not subscriptions

3 months if stable job 
6 months if variable income

---

## Slide 8

>> **Where to keep it?**

- A separate savings account
- A high-yield savings account (higher interest than a regular bank)
- A money market account (slightly higher rates, higher minimum balance)

The key: separate from your daily account

If it's easy to tap into, it gets spent

---

## Slide 9

>> **How to build it?**

1. Start with one month of salary. Cut spending, sell something, redirect a windfall
2. Automate. Set up a transfer the day after your salary arrives
3. Build toward the full target while also tackling high-interest debt

Even €100/month gets you to €1200 after a year

---

## Slide 10

>> **Is it an emergency?**

Ask: is it unexpected, urgent, AND necessary?

- Car broke down for work? Emergency
- Flight deal to Barcelona? Not an emergency
- Dental emergency? Emergency
- Phone upgrade? Not an emergency

---

## Slide 11

The emergency fund is the foundation

Without it, every other step, paying off debt, investing, building wealth, rests on unstable ground

One surprise expense and the whole plan collapses into debt

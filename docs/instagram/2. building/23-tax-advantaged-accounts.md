# Tax-Advantaged Accounts: Where to Hold Your Investments

## Caption

Where you hold an investment can matter as much as what you invest in. Tax-advantaged wrappers are the rare guaranteed uplift in personal finance, and most people use too few of them.

Every developed economy has them in roughly five flavors. Employer-matched retirement (free uplift). Tax-deferred personal retirement (pay tax later). Tax-free-growth personal retirement (pay tax now, never again). Purpose-specific accounts (health, education, first home). Equity-linked savings with lockups.

The vehicle names change by country. The decision logic doesn't.

The standard order: starter buffer in place, expensive debt under control, capture every available employer match, then fill tax-advantaged retirement before any taxable brokerage. Purpose-specific accounts where they match a real goal. Equity-linked last, where the lockup matches the horizon.

## Slide 3 (prose)

title: The single highest leverage move

Most employer plans match a percentage of what you contribute. 50% on the first 5% of salary doubles every euro in that range.

This is rare in personal finance: a guaranteed near-immediate return on contribution.

Once a starter buffer is in place and the most expensive debt is being addressed, capturing the full match is typically the next move.

---

## Slide 4 (comparison)

title: Pre-tax now or tax-free later

**Pre-tax in**

Contribute before income tax. Reduce today's tax bill. Pay income tax when you withdraw in retirement.

Often suits a high earner now, expecting a lower bracket later.

===

**Post-tax in**

Contribute already-taxed money. No deduction now. Future growth is fully tax-free on withdrawal.

Often suits early-career, lower brackets now, or anyone expecting future tax rates to rise.

## Slide 5 (list)

title: The standard order to fill them

1. Starter buffer in place; expensive debt under control
2. Full buffer rebuilt
3. Capture every available employer match
4. Max tax-advantaged retirement up to annual limits
5. Purpose-specific accounts aligned to real goals
6. Equity-linked only if the lockup matches your horizon
7. Taxable brokerage for everything that doesn't fit

---

## Slide 6 (prose)

title: The wrapper is not the investment

Opening a tax-advantaged account is half the work. You still have to choose what to put inside it.

A high-fee default fund inside a tax-advantaged wrapper can erase the tax advantage entirely.

Match the holdings inside to your asset allocation. The wrapper protects the growth. The fund determines what's growing.

# Beyond the Core: Commodities and Cryptocurrency as Satellite Assets

## Caption

Gold. Bitcoin. Oil. They come up in every investing conversation. Few belong in a core portfolio.

Commodities have no cash flow. The only return is the price moving up. Gold's job, over the long run, is to hold value during inflation and crises. Not to compound wealth.

Cryptocurrency has no cash flow either. No underlying business. No track record beyond 2009. Bitcoin has had multiple drawdowns of 70 to 85%. Position sizing should assume the holding can go to zero and you would still be fine.

Both belong, if at all, as small satellites around a core of stocks, bonds, real estate, and cash. Never as the foundation of a plan.

## Slide 2 (prose)

title: Commodities are a bet on price

A commodity is a physical good: gold, oil, coffee, copper. None of them produce cash. The only return is the price moving up.

Gold's role is not to compound wealth. It is to hold value during inflation and currency crises.

A 5 to 10% allocation is a common ceiling. Many investors hold none and are fine.

---

## Slide 3 (prose)

title: Cryptocurrency is closer to speculation than investment

No cash flow. No underlying business. Price determined entirely by what the next buyer is willing to pay.

Bitcoin has had multiple drawdowns of 70 to 85% in its history.

The honest position size: money you could lose entirely without changing your financial plan. For most people that is 1 to 5%, or zero.

---

## Slide 4 (comparison)

title: Where does the return actually come from

**Core**

Stocks, bonds, real estate, cash.

Returns come from underlying economic activity: profits, interest, rent. Cash is paid to you while you hold.

===

**Satellites**

Commodities, cryptocurrency.

Returns come purely from price movement. Nothing is paid to you while you hold. The next buyer is the entire mechanism.

---

## Slide 5 (list)

title: The mistakes that cost the most

1. Treating gold as a wealth engine, not a hedge
2. Confusing high volatility with high expected return
3. Borrowing to buy a no cash flow asset
4. Letting recent price action drive allocation
5. Holding crypto on a single exchange, no backup

## Slide 7 (prose)

title: The order of operations matters

Satellites only earn their slot once the core is doing its job. A diversified core across stocks, bonds, real estate, and cash is the prerequisite, not a parallel decision.

Without that, satellite allocation is just speculation dressed up as portfolio construction.

Build the core. Add satellites only if you still want to.

# Real Estate as an Investment: Beyond Just Owning a Home

## Beyond Just Owning a Home

## Caption

Property appreciated 30% over a decade. The owner's actual return is often half of that, sometimes less. Maintenance, taxes, insurance, vacancy, and transaction costs quietly take the rest.

Real estate is a legitimate asset class. It is also where popular intuition breaks down most dramatically, because the math hides in friction. Long run developed market real returns have been in the 3 to 6% range after costs, below stocks, above bonds.

Leverage is what makes real estate different. A 20% down payment on a property turns a 10% price move into a 50% move on your equity, in either direction. Wealth and wipeouts both run through that lever.

"Renting is throwing money away" is a myth. Rent buys shelter, flexibility, and risk transfer to the landlord. Ownership is shelter plus a financial position with monthly costs.

The right comparison is not rent vs buy. It is buying vs renting and investing the difference.

## Slide 4 (stat)

label: Put 20% down, then watch what a 10% move does
hero: +10% → +50%
caption: A 10% rise on the property becomes a 50% jump on your equity. The fall works the same way.
note: €60,000 down on a €300,000 place. Up 10%, your equity hits €90,000. Down 10%, it drops to €30,000. Down 20%, it's gone. That's leverage: it magnifies both directions.

source: Illustrative, before interest and fees. Leverage cuts both ways, up and down.

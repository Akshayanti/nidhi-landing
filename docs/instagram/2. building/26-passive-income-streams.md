# Passive Income Streams: Making Your Money Work Without You

## Caption

Real passive income is quieter than what gets sold online. The honest sources are dividends, interest, rent, and royalties. Not seven streams from a course.

Passive income is income you earn without trading time for it directly. That doesn't mean zero effort ever. It means effort invested upfront (saving, investing, building, creating), followed by income that persists without daily input.

The uncomfortable truth: meaningful passive income takes meaningful capital. Generating €1,000 a month at a 3% yield needs roughly €400,000 in invested assets. At 2% yield, closer to €600,000. Replacing a modest salary takes a portfolio measured in hundreds of thousands of euros, not months of effort.

Yield is not the same as total return. Chasing a high yield often means chasing distressed credit, return of capital dressed as income, or hidden risk. Focus on total return during accumulation. Reinvest dividends. Watch the engine compound.

## Slide 5 (comparison)

title: The number that fools almost everyone: yield
left_icon: percent
right_icon: trendUp

**Chasing yield**

A 6% payout on a fund going nowhere. You collect 6%, and the value just sits there.

===

**Total return**

A 2% payout on a fund growing 5%. That's 7%, and it keeps compounding.

## Slide 6 (list)

title: The traps dressed up to look like income

1. Chasing a yield far above the category average
2. Calling hands-on landlording "passive"
3. Counting income from a business that needs you
4. Forgetting how passive income gets taxed
5. Spending dividends instead of reinvesting them

## Slide 7 (prose)

title: One quiet decision does most of the work

Spent dividends are a one-off payday. Reinvested dividends buy more income-producing assets, which then pay you even more.

The compounding lands on the income itself, not just the principal.

Most of the gap between an early investor and a late one traces back to this one habit.

# Instagram Keyword & Hashtag Tuning Rationale

> Updated 2026-11-03: from Series 4 (Optimizing) on, each Instagram kit carries its own `angle:` lines and its `hashtags_day1`, `hashtags_day2`, `keywords_day1` and `keywords_day2` in frontmatter, so this file no longer stages per-post angles. The Series 2 and 3 record and the old staging notes are archived: see [done/IG-KEYWORDS.md](done/IG-KEYWORDS.md). What stays here are the tuning rules the kits still follow. Format context: reels only from Oct 29, 2026, with day 1 and day 2 linked as Part 1 and Part 2 (REELS-PIPELINE §1e).

Tuning rules live in `PLAYBOOK.md` §3 (hashtags) and §6 (keywords block); the rules below are the project-wide defaults that inform each kit's tuning.

- Every keyword passes the searchability test: "would a real person type this into IG search, in this language?" No filler.
- Per-post audience angle varies — each post in a series aims at a distinct sub-audience so IG's classifier doesn't anchor the account to one cluster.
- Hashtags and keywords don't duplicate each other — community-browse terms live in hashtags, search phrases + multilingual terms live in the keyword array.
- Multilingual coverage: EN base + targeted translations in languages where the native word is what people actually search (DE, FR, ES, IT, NL, PL, SV, NO, DA, CS). Cognates (`inflation`, `budget`, `euro`) aren't re-translated.
- Array size: 13–18 keywords per post (tight side of the 18–24 sweet spot documented in PLAYBOOK.md §6). Open item: kit 49 day 1 has 20 terms (Nov 3, 2026); lint or trim.

# Two-angle audit: Optimizing plus its Inclusive Finances companions

Inclusive Finances posts use their `inc-N` ids (renamed Oct 1, 2026; map in `blog-content-plan.md`, "Ids").

Audited Sep 30, 2026, against the drafts on disk (18 Optimizing posts in `src/content/blog/4. optimizing/`, 7 Inclusive Finances drafts in `docs/drafts/inclusive-finances/`).

## The test

The Instagram plan gives each blog post two days. Each day carries one angle, told twice: a reel for reach and a carousel for saves. A post passes only if it holds two angles that are:

1. **Distinct.** Day 2 must feel like a new topic to a follower, not the solution to day 1's problem or a continuation of it.
2. **Carryable.** Each angle has its own hook and either a number or a picture that a reel can be built around.
3. **Not a repeat.** The angle has not already been used by a nearby post in the run.

## Result

- **All 26 posts pass** as of Oct 1, 2026. Each has two distinct, carryable angles. (inc-2, shared households, was added to the run on Sep 30, 2026.)
- **inc-1 failed on Sep 30** as one idea plus its own checklist. Its draft now has a second idea, "Why the cheap fixes are usually enough" (three cost bands; most protection sits in the free one), which is distinct from the day 1 picture and carries its own visual.
- **4 more pass only with a specific choice of angle**, because the obvious second angle repeats a neighbour. They are marked "watch" below.

## Where the limits actually are

Idea count is not the constraint. These are.

**1. One post could not fill two days (resolved Oct 1, 2026).** inc-1 had a single idea. Options: run it as a one-day post (reel and carousel on the same day, next post starts a day early), or use day 2 for a second angle the draft would need to grow, such as "most of the fixes are free".

**2. Repeats across neighbouring posts.** Four clusters share an obvious angle, so only one post in each cluster may use it:
- "A range, not a single number": 43 (55 merged into it).
- "Real versus nominal": 43 and 54.
- "Sequence of returns": 43 (from the merged 55) and 60.
- "The five documents": inc-6 and inc-7, which ship two slots apart, and the audit checklist in inc-1.

**3. Reel visuals, not ideas, are the build cost.** The 52 angles need eight visual families. One is built to the new standard. Three more exist only as the basic scenes from the older reels (compare, list, flow) and would need lifting to the level of the post 43 chart.

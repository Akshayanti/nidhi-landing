# Usability sessions with readers (deferred)

**Status:** deferred by the owner on 2026-11-14. Order of work: the planner beta first, then this, once there is an audience to recruit from. Nothing here is started, and nothing here is scheduled.

## Why this exists

The council decided (design F2.5, Nov 2026, with PR #587) not to build a "Reading settings" panel until there is evidence that a reader needs one. The first trigger was "a reader report or a usability session". Stephanie's reservation: readers with ADHD, dyslexia or anxiety are the least likely to email about a barrier, so waiting for reports alone may never reopen the question.

The council (Claude, Codex, DeepSeek) agreed this amended rule on 2026-11-14:

> A Reading settings panel is not built now. The owner arranges at least one round of usability sessions that includes readers with ADHD, dyslexia, low vision and screen reader users, on their own devices, without asking anyone to disclose a diagnosis. One reproducible task barrier from a session or a reader report reopens the question. Reopening authorises investigation, not automatic construction. A control is never the substitute for a default fix.

When and how the sessions happen is the owner's decision.

## What the sessions would also settle

No review of the site so far used a real screen reader, a real phone, Windows High Contrast or a disabled reader. `/accessibility/` says so. A round of sessions is what would let that sentence change.

## Stephanie's minimal plan

- **Who:** five or six people, with at least one of each: a low-vision reader who uses zoom or large text, a dyslexic reader, an older reader on a phone, a screen reader user, and a reader in Hindi or Marathi.
- **Tasks**, the same for everyone:
  1. Read one lesson's summary and decide whether to read on.
  2. Compare two loans in the free tool.
  3. Find the Accessibility page and report a barrier.
- **Method:** their own devices and settings, thinking aloud. The observer watches and takes notes, and does not help or explain.
- **Rule for building the panel, set beforehand:** only if two or more participants try to change spacing, contrast or font and cannot do it with their own settings. (This is a higher bar than the council's "one reproducible case", which only reopens the question.)
- **A date is set when this plan is picked up**, so the trigger does not stay open-ended.

## Recruiting: the owner's direction so far

- **Not the beta waitlist.** The beta page promises one email, the invite. Beta sign-ups also chose the planner, not the site's lessons and tools, and nothing about the list selects for the readers needed. Beta users are the right people to ask about the planner's own usability once the beta is open; that is the planner side's question.
- **Through the volunteering call**, once the audience is larger: the volunteers page, its form and the Sunday volunteer post.

## What has to be decided or built before a call goes out

1. **The volunteers page is translation-only today** (title, lead and every task). A second kind of help needs adding, in all five languages: try the site while the owner watches, about 30 minutes.
2. **Ask by how people read, never by condition:** "I enlarge text", "I use a screen reader", "long pages are hard for me", "I mostly read on a phone". This keeps "You do not need to share a diagnosis" true.
3. **Privacy.** A "how I read" answer on the form is new data and can hint at a disability. It needs a privacy notice change and a changelog entry before the form changes; keep the answer optional and delete it after the sessions. Recordings or notes about named people are also personal data the notice does not cover today; notes without names avoid that.
4. **The volunteer form is English only**, a barrier listed on `/accessibility/` (#487). It matters more here, since one participant should be a Hindi or Marathi reader.
5. **What participants get.** Volunteers already get credit and an optional beta invite; decide whether the same applies.
6. **If the call brings fewer than three suitable people**, the next step is communities that exist for such asks, not the beta list.

## Process when this is picked up

The volunteers page change and the Sunday caption are copy and design changes: council through the three design stages, Stephanie's sign-off, five languages, a What's changed item, and the privacy entry in point 3.

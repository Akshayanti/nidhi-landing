## Day 2 (angle)

angle: Change one thing at a time
number: The whole move adds €200 a month. Inside it, the cheaper city adds €600 and the pay cut takes back €400.
takeaway: When a decision bundles several changes, run each one alone, then together. One of them usually does the work.
tool: The one at a time test. Each change alone, then all together, then compare the results.

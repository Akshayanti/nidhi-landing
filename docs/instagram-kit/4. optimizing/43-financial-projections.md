## Brief

person: Eva, 32
situation: Has €50,000 invested and adds €1,000 a month. A calculator told her one number for age 62, and a simulation told her 90%.
withheld: How to choose a growth assumption, the three ways people set it too high, why real returns and not nominal, and why the order of returns matters once you draw money out.

number: Eva's plan run 10,000 times on past yearly swings: in 90% of runs her pot passed about €510,000. That is the model's estimate under its assumptions, not a promise about her future.

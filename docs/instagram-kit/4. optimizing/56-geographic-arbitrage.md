## Brief

person: Marek, 33
situation: Marek works remotely and earns the same wherever he lives. He is comparing his city with a place where the same life costs half.
withheld: Visa routes, how tax residency is tested country by country, healthcare, currency moves, the costs no spreadsheet shows, and common routes region by region.

## Day 1 (angle)

angle: Same pay, half the living costs: why could that cut working years by more than half?
breadth: narrow | Needs a viewer whose pay could travel with them
number: Spending €48,000 a year needs a pot of €1.2 million; €24,000 needs €600,000; saved share 40% becomes 70%; about 22 years becomes about 9. The reel says the two pots and the two year counts
takeaway: Lower living costs work twice: more of the same pay is saved each year, and the pot needed, a multiple of spending, is smaller.
tool: The two effects. What lower costs do to the yearly saving, to the pot needed, and what the sum leaves out.
send_to: Someone weighing a move abroad.
send_why: number

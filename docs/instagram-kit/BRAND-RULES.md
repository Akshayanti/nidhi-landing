# Brand rules for every nidhi content piece

One page checklist for anyone producing nidhi content (people, Claude, Codex). It summarises rules that already exist; the source of each is in brackets. If a rule here and its source disagree, the source wins and this sheet gets fixed.

## Who it is for

- Global expats from anywhere, living anywhere except North America. Europe is the largest segment, money in €, examples rotate across expat hubs. No US or Canada targeting, no US-only products, tax or culture. Don't frame the account as single-country (EU-only or India-only). [PLAYBOOK §1]
- Write for a reader new to the topic, often anxious about money ("Eva-type"). Problem first, plain words, one idea at a time. A chart that reads as math homework makes things worse. [PLAYBOOK §8, blog-content-plan Rule 9]

## Honesty

- nidhi is being built and is not out yet. Always describe it as being built ("the person building nidhi"), never as available. No promised features or dates. The free tools at nidhi.today/free/ are live and may be pointed to wherever they help. [blog-content-plan, Oct 1 update; PLAYBOOK §2.1]
- No product plug inside the teaching. One fixed maker's line, with no ask attached. [PLAYBOOK §2.1]
- Never invent facts about the founder beyond what the beliefs page says. Examples and characters are illustrative and must not be presented as real records.
- Every number is computed from stated inputs, and the post, carousel, reel and blog must agree with each other. Show the working: assumptions on the frame, ranges over single numbers. [PLAYBOOK §2.1]
- The reel voice and script are AI assisted, and the caption says so.

## Financial compliance (MiFID II, CNB)

- No named investment products, funds, tickers, coins or platforms. No product against product comparisons by name. [PLAYBOOK §13 MiFID-safe directive]
- No forecasts presented as fact, no return guarantees. Any number that depends on a return assumption says "illustrative" and its assumption inside the image or frame itself, not only in the caption. [PLAYBOOK §13]
- No personalised or jurisdiction specific tax math, rates or limits. [PLAYBOOK §13, blog-content-plan bright lines]
- No advice phrasing ("you should buy", "now is the time"). Present options, use "a common guideline is". Direct behavioural teaching is fine; the bright lines are narrow. [blog-content-plan General Rules and Directive philosophy]
- No before and after performance of a real product, no screenshots of real brokerage or banking apps. [PLAYBOOK §13]

## Inclusion and accessibility

- Situation and topic hashtags only, never identity tags. Inclusive posts stay interleaved after their host post. [PLAYBOOK §3, blog-content-plan]
- Colour is never the only carrier of meaning: label what the colour means. Text must be legible at phone size, with strong contrast. Every image gets alt text. [PLAYBOOK §13 checklist; beliefs page]
- No US-only cultural references, no country stereotypes, no AI generated images as final assets, no memes inside educational carousels. [PLAYBOOK §13]

## Engagement

- One ask per post: save, share, tag, or a self assessment poll. No "comment X and I'll DM you", no reply bait, no manual engagement asks. [PLAYBOOK §4, §7]
- Hooks are cold: never assume the viewer saw an earlier post. Name the problem, keep the curiosity gap open, don't leak the answer. [PLAYBOOK §8, REELS-PIPELINE]

## Format and typography

- No em dashes, en dashes, hyphens or double hyphens anywhere a reader sees: slides, captions, stories, on-screen text. Use commas, colons or "to" for ranges. [PLAYBOOK §8]
- No links in Instagram captions; say "link in bio". [PLAYBOOK §6]
- Hashtags: at most 5, always #nidhi, none from the banned list, none repeated between consecutive posts. Keywords: 18 to 24 on carousels, 13 to 18 on reels, never duplicating a hashtag. [PLAYBOOK §3, §6]
- Carousels 4:5 at up to 8 slides; stories 9:16 with content clear of the top 260px and bottom 360px; reels 9:16 at about −14 LUFS. [PLAYBOOK §2.1, §5, §7; REELS-PIPELINE]

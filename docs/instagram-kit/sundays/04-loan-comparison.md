## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

Two offers for a €20,000 loan over five years. One at 6.0% with no fee, one at 5.5% with an €800 fee.

The 5.5% offer has the lower monthly payment: €382.02 against €386.66. Once its €800 fee is counted, it also costs €522 more in total.

The free loan comparison at nidhi.today puts two to five offers side by side: monthly payment, payoff time, total interest, total paid, and an APR that folds the fee into one rate you can compare. Here: 6.00% against 7.20%.

APR rules differ from country to country, so treat the tool's figure as a comparison, not a quote. The lender's offer sheet is the binding number.

The calculations run in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent. It also reads in Hindi and Spanish.

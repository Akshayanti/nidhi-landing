## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

€10,000 kept as cash for ten years buys about €7,441 of today's shopping, if prices rise 3% a year. At 2% it is €8,203; at 4%, €6,756.

In a savings account paying 2.5% while prices rise 3%, it still buys less: €9,525.

That is one of four questions the free inflation calculator at nidhi.today answers: what money buys later, what a price costs later, what a past amount is worth today, and whether a raise or a savings rate keeps up.

An answer at a rate you type also shows the same sum one point lower and higher, because a rate is a scenario, not a forecast. For the past, it uses each country's actual yearly inflation, from the World Bank, for 193 countries and the euro area.

The calculations run in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent. It also reads in Hindi and Spanish.

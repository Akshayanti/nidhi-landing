## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

Eva lives in Prague and spends crowns. Her savings are in euros, an old workplace pension is in pounds, and the car loan is in crowns.

Added up in crowns, at illustrative rates, that is 658,500 CZK. But more than 95% of it sits in currencies she doesn't spend, so a move in the euro or the pound can change that total noticeably, whatever she does in Prague.

That is what the free net worth calculator at nidhi.today shows: one total in the currency you choose, from 29 currencies at the European Central Bank's daily rates, and how much of it sits outside the currency you spend. It works for one currency too.

Whether that share matters depends on where you will spend the money later. The tool shows the size; the judgement stays yours.

The calculations run in your browser; the numbers you type are not sent. It also reads in Hindi and Spanish.

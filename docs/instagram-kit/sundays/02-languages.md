## Brief

purpose: A call for volunteers (owner, Oct 23, 2026). nidhi reads in five languages; the owner translates the site with AI and wants native or near native speakers of Spanish, Czech and Marathi (a financial background is more than welcome) (Hindi is covered) to review the site's translations and to help translate the lessons, which stay English for now because the owner can't quality check them alone. Native speakers of the next roadmap languages (not open yet) may leave their contact to be reached when one starts. What volunteers get (owner's choices): a say in their language's words (the edition's glossary), credit on the site under the name they choose (opt in, several people per language, removable on request; where it appears is decided when it is built, with its privacy changelog entry), and an invite to the planner's beta when it opens (no date). No time commitment is mentioned. Founder voice "I", never gendered. Contact: the form at nidhi.today/volunteers/ (the Instagram bio link while the call runs), or an Instagram DM.
why: Owner's reason, in the founder's voice: nidhi should work for more people, so it covers more languages, and help is welcome. The order of the next languages is docs/i18n/locale-roadmap.md (owner's rule, Oct 20, 2026): ranked by how many people read the language, at most two editions per region. No dates are given because there are none.
honesty: Matches the site: LOCALES are en, hi, es, cs and mr; /hi/, /es/, /cs/ and /mr/ carry the site's pages and the four tools under /free/; lessons are English only (LessonsInEnglish). Amount formats per locale are in the roadmap's "What the published locales settled" (lakh and crore for Hindi and Marathi, `1 058 721 Kč` for Czech). Reader figures for upcoming languages are the roadmap's rounded estimates from speaker counts and literacy rates. The net worth terms are each glossary's entry. Translations were made with AI help and reviewed by the three-model council, with no native human reviewer, hence the disclosure. hello@nidhi.today is the site's public contact address. No identity, country or festival hashtags, no flags (PLAYBOOK §3 cohort hygiene). Language names in their own script, as the site's picker shows them (LOCALE_META). The call-for-help lines follow each glossary: Hindi आप, Spanish tú, Czech vykání without gendered participles, Marathi तुम्ही.

## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

Financial literacy is everyone's right, and I need your help to make it that way.

I drafted nidhi's Spanish, Czech and Marathi with AI and can't tell if they sound right. If you speak one of them natively, or nearly so, help me check them and, if you'd like, translate the lessons too (English only for now). A financial background is welcome, not needed.

Why it matters to me: only about 1 in 5 people speak English (about 1.5 billion of 8.1 billion, per Ethnologue 2025). Money help should reach the other four, in their own language.

In return: a say in the glossary words for your language, credit on the site if you want it, and an invite when the planner's beta opens.

More languages are coming (last card). No dates yet: sign up for yours now and I'll reach out when work on it starts.

→ Sign up at nidhi.today/volunteers/ (link in bio)

No ads, no data selling. Free tools you can use today, link in bio.

## Brief

purpose: Beliefs 6. No ads, no data selling, no retargeting. Says plainly what is counted, so "no asterisks" is literally true.
honesty: Matches the privacy notice: no ad tech, no data sold, no cross site tracking, no retargeting. The site does count anonymous pageviews, and the post says so rather than claiming no tracking at all.

## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

Your finances are not ad inventory.

nidhi doesn't run ads. It doesn't sell your data. It doesn't do the creepy thing where you search for one money topic and get followed around the internet by ads for it.

What it does count, without asterisks: anonymous pageviews, to see what helps. Clicks only if you say yes on the banner. The privacy page lists every detail and every change, dated.

Money is sensitive enough. A finance product shouldn't need to make money from your worry.

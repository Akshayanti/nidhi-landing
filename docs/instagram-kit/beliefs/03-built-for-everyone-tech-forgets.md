## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

A money tool that leaves people out is not neutral.

Money decisions are stressful enough. The page shouldn't make them harder because you use a screen reader, see colour differently, or take in information differently, for example through ADHD or dyslexia.

So on nidhi today: charts carry their numbers in text for screen readers and don't rely on colour alone, and every lesson opens with a short summary. It isn't finished, and I'd like to hear where it falls short.

→ Share this with someone who has given up on a finance app that wasn't built for them

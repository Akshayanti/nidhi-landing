## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

A rule of thumb is a starting point, not an answer.

Take the common one for emergency funds: 3 to 6 months of costs. It is a useful place to start. It doesn't say why that range, when it is too little or more than you need, or what changes it for you.

So where a lesson gives a rule of thumb, it also explains why it exists, where it breaks, and what depends on you: your country, your income, your debts, how far ahead you are planning. I would rather you understand the reasoning than follow a rule that was never written for your life.

## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

The lessons are free, forever.

No paywall. No "subscribe for the good stuff". No lesson that teaches half the idea and keeps the answer behind a gate.

If a lesson helps you make a better decision, even if you never use the product, that isn't a failed funnel. That is the point. Tell me about it; that's the kind of fuel this runs on.

Free doesn't mean casual: the lessons are a learning path, built in order, with their sources cited.

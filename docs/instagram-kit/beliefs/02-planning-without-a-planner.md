## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

Financial planning shouldn't require a financial planner for every ordinary question.

Can I afford this flat? What happens if I take a career break? When could I stop working? Those questions still deserve care. But the math behind them should not be hidden behind jargon, upsells or a black box.

Some questions do need a professional: tax filing where you live, a will that holds up locally, anything regulated. A good tool makes the rest clear first, so the time with an expert goes further.

That is the standard I'm building nidhi to: show the math, name the assumptions, leave the choice to you. It isn't out yet; the lessons and free tools are.

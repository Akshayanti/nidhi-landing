## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

Three partners share a home. If one dies without a will, a partner may inherit nothing.

Marriage switches on protections automatically: inheritance with no will, often a survivor pension, medical decisions. In a household of three or more partners, one partner can hold that slot. Every other partner is covered only by what is written down: wills, beneficiary forms, healthcare proxies. And some things no document extends: joint tax treatment, a partner visa, legal parenthood.

Slide 5 is the one to keep: three questions for a household with one legal slot.

## Day 2 (carousel)

### Caption

Three partners, one pension form. Many forms can name all three, with a share for each.

Beneficiary forms on pensions, insurance and investments often take several names, with a percentage for each. Hospital visit forms can usually list several people. A healthcare proxy or a financial power of attorney names one person at a time, so households often agree an order of alternates. And one form looks open and often is not: the ongoing survivor pension, which many schemes pay to a legal spouse only.

Slide 5 is the one to keep: three checks for every form.

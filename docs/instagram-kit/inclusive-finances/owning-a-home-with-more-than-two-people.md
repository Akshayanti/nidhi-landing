## Day 2 (carousel)

### Caption

On a shared mortgage, everyone named owes all of it. Not their share.

A home shared by a group runs on three documents. The title says who owns. The loan says who owes, and lenders often cap it at two to four names. Only an agreement between the owners records what each person put in and what happens when someone leaves. When one owner leaves, the others usually pay out the share and need a new loan on fewer incomes. If they cannot, the house may have to be sold.

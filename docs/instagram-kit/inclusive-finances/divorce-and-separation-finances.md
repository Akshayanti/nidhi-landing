It cuts both ways: a pension also grows untaxed in the meantime, and a home is worth more to someone who needs to live in it.

## Day 2 (carousel)

### Caption

A divorce settlement divides the assets. It does not update a single form.

A pension or life insurance form can still name a former partner, and in many systems a divorce does not cancel it; the form often overrides a will. A former partner may still be executor, heir or the person with power of attorney. And a joint loan stays joint: the lender did not sign the settlement, and can still pursue either person for the full amount.

## Brief

person: Alma, Ben, Chidi and Dana, flatmates, illustrative
situation: Four adults share one flat. Net monthly incomes of €5,000, €3,000, €2,000 and €1,000, €11,000 in total. Shared costs of €3,200 a month: €2,400 rent, €300 utilities, €500 food.

angle: An equal split is equal in euros and very unequal in weight: 16% of one income, 80% of another

takeaway: No split is the right one. Working out each share as a percentage of each income shows what a method asks, and of whom.

## Day 2 (carousel)

### Caption

One flatmate moves out. The rent does not move with her.

Four adults share €3,200 of costs, 29% of their €11,000 income. If Alma, the top earner, leaves and her room stays empty, rent and utilities stay at €2,700 and food falls to about €375. That is €3,075 on €6,000 of income, 51%. She paid €1,455; the three who stay save €125 on food and have to find €1,330 a month until someone new moves in.

Slide 5 is the one to keep: the exit test, run for every person in the household.

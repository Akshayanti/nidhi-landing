## Day 1 (carousel)

### Caption

Most home buying advice starts with "compare the rate". For many people, interest is ruled out.

Interest free home finance reaches the same end (you live in the home now and own it later) through one of three structures. A cost plus sale (murabaha): the financier buys the flat and sells it to you at a higher price, disclosed and fixed up front. A lease to own (ijara): the financier owns it and leases it to you, and ownership transfers through a separate sale or gift in the contract, commonly at the end. A shrinking partnership (diminishing musharakah): you buy the home together and you buy their share bit by bit. Monthly costs are often benchmarked to market rates, so they can look similar. The contract is what differs.

## Day 2 (carousel)

### Caption

Shares can fit an interest free plan. Which companies is the work.

A share is part ownership of a business, with profit and loss shared, which is why investing in shares is generally considered permissible. Screening comes in two layers. First, what the company does: conventional banking and insurance, alcohol, gambling and pork products are typically excluded. Second, how it is financed: common tests cap interest bearing debt and cash at around a third each, and prohibited income at around 5%. The exact ratios differ between standards.

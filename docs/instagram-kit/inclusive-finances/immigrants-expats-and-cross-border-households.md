Twenty years of paying every bill on time. Move countries, and a lender sees nothing at all.

## Day 2 (carousel)

### Caption

Same €300 sent home every month. Over ten years, the provider can make a €1,620 difference.

At 6.5% per transfer, around the global average, the costs come to €2,340 over ten years. At 2%, a price some competitive digital providers charge, they come to €720. The gap is more than five months of the transfer itself. Much of the cost hides in the exchange rate, not the fee.

alt: The route matters more than the brand: a busy corridor with many providers is often cheap; a thin corridor with few options is often expensive; the same company can be cheap on one route and dear on another.

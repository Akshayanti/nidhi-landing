Together for years, not married? In many places, the law can treat your partner as a stranger.

Marriage switches on a bundle of financial defaults the day you sign: inheritance without a will, medical decisions, a legal regime for shared property, often joint tax. Living together may create no automatic inheritance or decision rights, however long you have been together. A will, a record of how you own your home, a cohabitation agreement and powers of attorney can make the household's intentions visible. Almost every piece can be rebuilt on purpose, and most of it cheaply.

## Day 2 (carousel)

### Caption

Twelve years paying half the mortgage. In many countries, legally, that was generosity.

Sofia and Lena, an illustrative couple, have been together twelve years. The flat is in Lena's name; Sofia has paid half of every mortgage payment. Neither has a will. If Lena dies, in many countries the flat passes to her parents and Sofia has no automatic right to stay. A will, a recorded ownership share and powers of attorney could have changed much of what followed. Formalising an ownership share may need legal advice, lender consent and extra costs.
